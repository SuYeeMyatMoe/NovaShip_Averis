# P2, Case Assistant, Policy and AI Safety Lead

You own everything that talks to a person: Ask AI (with RAG over the case data and the knowledge folder), translation, the policy explanation, the Notify Party / share message, batch explanations, and the guardrails. Rule: **the assistant never invents a value, never sends anything, never treats the extracted Notify Party as permission to contact anyone, never shows another case.**

Read first: [AGENT.md](AGENT.md) sections 3, 4 and 8.

## If something is wrong
| Symptom | Look at | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Assistant names a mismatch the card does not show | `_llm_answer` post-check returns None | fix the rule branch, never the comparator |
| Knowledge answer is off-topic | local hashing embeddings are keyword-only | switch to Gemini/OpenAI embeddings and rebuild |
| Translation changed a company name | `_PROTECT` regex | add the token shape |
| "read-only" on Policies | not ADMIN | pick `Syed Faraz Ali` in the header |
